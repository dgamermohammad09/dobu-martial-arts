ABOUT DOBU MARTIAL ARTS

DoBu Martial Arts is a premier martial arts gym and fitness facility located on Mecca Street in Amman, Jordan. We offer structured classes across four major martial arts disciplines, as well as functional fitness training and specialized self-defence courses. Our gym is equipped with a large matted martial arts area, a fully-equipped fitness gym, a sauna, a steam room, and modern changing and shower facilities. Whether you are training for fitness, self-defence, or competitive martial arts, our accredited black-belt instructors guide every session in a safe and supportive environment.

OUR MARTIAL ARTS DISCIPLINES

GRAPPLING

JIU-JITSU

Ground fighting and submission grappling focusing on leverage, positional control, joint locks, and chokes. Suitable for all fitness levels.

Instructors: Mauricio Gomez (3rd Dan) & Guy Victory (2nd Dan)

VIEW JIU-JITSU TIMES
STRIKING

MUAY THAI

The traditional "Art of Eight Limbs" utilizing punches, kicks, elbows, knees, and clinch dynamics for powerful striking and high-intensity cardiovascular endurance.

Instructors: Mauricio Gomez & Morris Davis (Accredited)

VIEW MUAY THAI TIMES
TRADITIONAL

KARATE

Shotokan karate emphasizing linear striking, blocking, stance work, and kata mastery. Structured belt grading progression from beginner yellow to black belt.

Instructors: Sarah Nova (5th Dan) & Morris Davis (3rd Dan)

VIEW KARATE TIMES
OLYMPIC COMBAT

JUDO

Olympic combat sport using opponent momentum for dynamic throws, foot sweeps, pins, and breakfalls (ukemi) to develop athletic power, balance, and core stability.

Instructors: Mauricio Gomez (4th Dan) & Guy Victory (1st Dan)
