MEMBERSHIP FAQS

Everything you need to know before training at DoBu.

Do I need prior martial arts experience or high fitness to join? ▼
No! Over 60% of our new members start with zero martial arts background. Our instructors adapt every drill to your current fitness level, ensuring you train in a supportive, safe environment without pressure.
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or pause my membership? ▼
Yes. You can upgrade or change your membership plan at any time through your online Member Portal dashboard. There are no cancellation penalties or hidden lock-ins.
What equipment or clothing do I need for my first class? ▼
For your initial session, standard athletic gym clothing (t-shirt and shorts/joggers without zips) is perfect. We have sanitized loaner boxing gloves and shin guards available. Traditional gis can be purchased at member discount from our pro shop once enrolled.
How does the 6-Week Beginners' Self-Defence Course operate? ▼
The course runs two 1-hour sessions per week over 6 weeks (12 hours total instruction). It covers situational awareness, breakaways from wrist/hair/choke grabs, verbal de-escalation, and ground recovery techniques. Certificate awarded upon completion.
